How It All Started

It started with a simple craving for brownies and no oven in sight. We thought, why not try the stovetop? Using a unique pot-in-pot method, our very first batch came out surprisingly perfect—dense, rich, and ultra-fudgy.

Today, Stovetop Bakery uses that exact same technique to bring you freshly baked artisan treats daily. Our mission is simple: high-quality ingredients, zero shortcuts, and a lot of passion in every single bite.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do the brownies stay fresh? +

Our stovetop brownies stay fresh for up to 5 days when stored in an airtight container at room temperature. You can also microwave them for 10 seconds for that fresh-out-of-the-pot gooeyness!

Do you offer gluten-free options? +

Currently, we focus on our classic wheat-based recipe to perfect the stovetop method, but we are working on a delicious gluten-free variant coming soon.

How does the pot-in-pot method work? +

We place a smaller baking pan inside a larger heavy-bottomed pot on the stove. The large pot traps heat exactly like an oven, baking the brownies slowly to retain maximum moisture and fudginess.
